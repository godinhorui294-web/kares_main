Dynamic Mission Planning
Algorithm for UAV Formation
in Battleﬁeld Environment
JING ZHANG
YANI CUI
JIA REN
Hainan University, Haikou, China
The limited time duration and spatial instability of TSTs increase
the difﬁculty of mission planning for unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV)
formations, which in turn affects the task execution abilities of UAVs.
Aiming at this problem, a multi-UAV dynamic mission planning al-
gorithm based on the time window mechanism is proposed under
a distributed formation structure. The proposed algorithm compre-
hensively considers a variety of complex constraints involved in ac-
tual application scenarios and constructs a UAV formation mission
planning model with minimum task execution cost and path plan-
ning cost as the optimization goal. In this article, particle swarm
optimization algorithm is improved by introducing a speciﬁc particle
coding method and a competitive co-evolutionary population updating
strategy, to enhance the solution speed and accuracy of the mission
planning model. In this way, the optimal task allocation strategy and
task execution path are obtained to meet the mission requirements,
and to achieve the multi-UAV cooperative reconnaissance, strike and
evaluation of multiple TSTs. Furthermore, the effectiveness of the
proposed algorithm is validated via simulations.

I.
INTRODUCTION
Unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) has become the ‘air
darling’ of modern warfare and the “invisible razor edge”
of future battleﬁeld because of its low cost, good maneu-
verability, and strong stealth. While facing an increasingly
complex battleﬁeld environment, a single UAV faces difﬁ-
culties in meeting the needs of multi-target combat missions
with restriction factors, such as the load and combat range.
Therefore, multiple UAVs in coordination are required to
efﬁciently complete these tasks [1], [2], [3], [4], and the
premise of multi-UAV cooperative combat is an effective
mission planning strategy [5]. Multi-UAV cooperative mis-
sion planning refers to obtaining reasonable task allocation
and trajectory formulation of UAV formation according to
the battleﬁeld situation, target state, and attribute of each
UAV, to maximize the task execution efﬁciency. Therefore,
multi-UAV cooperative mission planning can be decom-
posed into two parts, namely multi-UAV cooperative task
allocation and multi-UAV cooperative path planning [6],
[7]. Speciﬁcally, multi-UAV cooperative task allocation
corresponds to assigning one or more orderly tasks to UAVs
within the formation based on the speciﬁc environmental
information and task requirements, under the constraints
of UAV attribute, to avoid resource conﬂicts and wastage
and ensure that each UAV performs the tasks at a minimum
cost [8]. On the other hand, multi-UAV cooperative path
planning refers to formulating the task execution path for
eachUAVundertheconstraintsofUAV’sperformance,time
coordination, and space coordination, according to the task
allocation results [9].
However, with the continuous emergence of new targets
in the battleﬁeld environment and increasing uncertainty of
the targets, the effectiveness of traditional mission plan-
ning algorithms can be insufﬁcient, especially in combat
missions involving time-sensitive targets (TSTs). TST is a
target that must be found, located, identiﬁed, attacked, and
evaluated within a limited time window [10]. According
to the maneuverability of the target, it can be divided into
two categories, where one is a moving TST, such as a
mobile missile launch system, a missile launch vehicle, an
unmanned surface vehicle, and an underwater submarine.
Meanwhile, the other is a ﬁxed TST, such as an intermit-
tently open radar station and an erected missile launcher.
The uncertainty of the duration window of TST is the
key difference between a TST and other types of targets,
which increases the difﬁculty of multi-UAV cooperative
reconnaissance, strike, and evaluation of TSTs. The lim-
ited duration and spatial instability of TSTs complicates
the UAV cooperative mission planning with the following
challenges:
1) The topology of UAV formation network changes
dynamically, and thus real-time and stable commu-
nication between the UAVs is very important.
2) Affected by the time window of TST, the temporal
coupling relationship between the formation tasks

II.
RELATED WORK
Multi-UAV cooperative mission planning is a typical
multi-constraint combinatorial optimization problem, and
the process can be divided into two parts: modeling and
calculation. In multi-UAV cooperative mission planning,
ﬁrstly, according to the battleﬁeld environment and task re-
quirements, the problem of multi-UAV cooperative mission
planning should be reasonably abstracted, the optimization
objectives and constraints should be deﬁned, and a reason-
able mission planning model should be established. Fol-
lowing the model development, an appropriate optimization
method can be used to dynamically solve the constructed
mission planning model.
There exist various methods to model the multi-UAV
cooperative mission planning such as: multiple travelling
salesman problem model; vehicle routing problem model;
networkﬂowoptimizationmodel;resourcewelfareproblem
model; mixed integer linear programming (MILP) model,
etc. [11], [12], [13], [14]. Among them, the MILP is a
popular modeling method, which transforms the multi-
UAV cooperative mission planning problem into an integer
programming problem, where various constraints can be
introduced [15]. Likewise, in [16], under the constraints of
task timing and task time window, a multi-UAV cooperative
mission planning model was constructed based on the MILP
model. In addition, aiming at the high dynamics, strong
uncertainty, and multiconstraint problems faced by hetero-
geneous agent path planning in complex environments, a
heterogeneous agent path planning model was constructed
based on the MILP model [17].
Depending on the features of constructed multi-UAV
cooperative mission planning model, it is necessary to use
an appropriate optimization algorithm to solve the mission
planning model. In this regard, there exist many kinds of
optimization methods for solving the multi-UAV coopera-
tive mission planning model. According to the formation
control mode of UAV, the optimization methods for solving
the cooperative mission planning model can be divided
into centralized mission planning algorithm and distributed
mission planning algorithm. Among them, the centralized
mission planning algorithm can generate better solutions
than the distributed mission planning algorithm and ensures
a smaller computational load for each UAV. However, the
optimal solution depends on the central node and its effec-
tive communication with each UAV. Alternatively, to make
full use of the autonomous abilities of each UAV to improve
theefﬁciencyofsolutionandavoidthedefectsofcentralized
solution framework, such as a large load of the central node
and poor robustness of the system, the distributed mission
planning algorithm has gradually become the prime focus
of academic attention [18], [19], [20]. In distributed control
mode, effective message transmission and data exchange
are needed among the UAVs to achieve the consensus of
formation task allocation and maximize the formation task
execution efﬁciency. Based on how the UAVs in a formation
reach a consensus, the distributed mission planning algo-
rithm can be divided into two categories, namely, distributed
mission planning algorithm based on the consistency of
situation information and distributed mission planning al-
gorithm based on the task consistency. The former that ﬁrst
allocates the tasks and then attempts to reach a consensus.
Speciﬁcally, each UAV in the formation ﬁrst uses its ob-
servation information for task allocation and then realizes
the consensus of task allocation results through information
interaction within the formation (the content of interactive
information is the task allocation result, task execution
cost, or beneﬁt of each UAV) [21]. On the other hand, the
mission planning algorithm based on situation information
consistency is a consensus redistribution algorithm. First,
information is shared within the formation (the interactive
information content is the observation information obtained
by each UAV sensor), and then the UAVs fuse the received
observation information to make the situation information
in the formation reach a consensus. Relying on this concept,
heuristic algorithms such as genetic algorithm (GA), PSO,
ant colony optimization (ACO), simulated annealing (SA)
and binarized neural network (BNN) can be used to solve

V.
SOLVING UAV FORMATION MISSION PLANNING
MODEL
In the standard PSO algorithm, the position vector of
each particle corresponds to a potentially feasible solution
to the optimization problem, and multiple particles form a
population. In the process of particle population evolution,
each particle updates its velocity vector and position vector
by learning the global optimal particle. However, when the
global optimal particle falls into the local optimum, the
entire population particle will also fall into the local opti-
mum,leadingtoprematureconvergenceofthealgorithm.To
improve the diversity of the population and avoid premature
convergence, the PSO algorithm is improved. First, the
mapping relationship between the position vector and the
solution of the mission planning model is established by a
speciﬁc particle coding method, and the feasible particles
are generated by modifying the position vector based on the
saturation mechanism. Second, by modifying the particle
speed and position updating formula, the particles update
their speeds and positions through learning objects. Finally,
the search direction adjustment mechanism is introduced
to judge the evolution state of the learning object, and the
search direction of the particle is adjusted at the appropriate
time to improve the diversity of the population. At the same
time, the cooperative evolution strategy is introduced to
select the learning object competitively, and the learning
object is dynamically generated and updated in the iterative
process of the algorithm to avoid particles falling into local
optima.

VII.
CONCLUSION
This article solve the problem of the dynamic allocation
of multiple UAVs for multiple time-sensitive tasks, but there
are still some shortcomings. Given the research content of
this article, the problems worthy of further discussion and
research include the following.
1) The application scenarios of this article are com-
pleted when the task execution information is com-
pletely complete, that is, it is assumed that the ob-
servation information obtained by the UAV partic-
ipating in the task is complete. However, due to
the performance limitations of sensor devices and
communication devices carried by UAVs and the
randomness and uncertainty of transmission delay
and packet loss, the information obtained by UAVs
is incomplete. Therefore, one of the key research
directions in the next step is to study the cooperative
mission planning of multiple UAVs under incom-
plete information conditions.
2) The performance of multi-UAV communication net-
work has an important inﬂuence on the effect of
formation cooperative task execution. This article
only considers the inﬂuence of network topology
on multi-UAV cooperative task execution, but does
not consider the inﬂuence of communication mode
and channel access mode on multi-UAV coopera-
tive task execution. Therefore, the next key research
direction is to combine the communication simula-
tion software to study the performance of the UAV
communication network and its impact on the multi-
UAVcooperativemissionplanning,andonthisbasis,
improve and expand the algorithm proposed in this
article.

七、结论
本文解决了多无人机对多时效性任务的动态分配
问题，但仍存在一些不足。结合本文的研究内容，值
得进一步讨论和研究的问题包括以下几个方面。
1)本文的应用场景是在任务执行信息完全完整时
完成的，即假设参与任务的无人机获得的观测信
息是完整的。然而，由于无人机所携带的传感器
设备和通信设备的性能限制，以及传输延迟和数
据包丢失的随机性和不确定性，无人机获得的信
息是不完整的。因此，下一步的一个关键研究方
向是研究在信息不完全条件下多无人机的协同任
务规划。
2)多无人机通信网络的性能对编队协同任务执行效
果有重要影响。本文仅考虑了网络拓扑对多无人机
协同任务执行的影响，但未考虑通信模式和信道接
入方式对多无人机协同任务执行的影响。因此，下
一步的关键研究方向是结合通信仿真软件研究无人
机通信网络的性能及其对多无人机协同任务规划的
影响，并在此基础上改进和扩展本文提出的算法。
